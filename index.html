The Purity Promise

How to know you're getting the best.

💧

Water Test

Real saffron releases its color slowly. Fake saffron turns water immediately red.

👃

Aroma

Sweet, floral, and earthy. If it smells like nothing, it is likely dyed corn silk.

👅

Taste

Authentic Kashmiri saffron is slightly bitter, never sweet.

📜

Lab Tested

Every batch is tested for crocin (color), picrocrocin (flavor), and safranal (aroma).
